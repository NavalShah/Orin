A LITTLE CONTEXT

What travels with the melody?

We keep the shape and movement of the phrase as we map it to piano. The original ornaments and expressive pitch bends are part of the Carnatic performance; this first sketch simplifies them so you can find the notes on a keyboard.

MUSICAL IDEAS

Hear the shape, then the details.

This sketch maps a short sargam-style phrase onto nearby piano notes. It keeps the up-and-down contour so a pianist can explore the melody, while simplifying features that need a more nuanced notation.

01Pitch movement

Follow the phrase as it rises and settles.

02Rhythmic feel

Notice the repeating pulse underneath the melody.

03Ornamentation

Small slides and turns give a performance its voice.

This is an educational sketch, not a definitive transcription. A musician’s interpretation brings details that a piano roll cannot capture.
